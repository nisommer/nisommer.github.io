# Privacy

This personal website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, pages viewed, approximate country and referring websites. Analytics loads automatically unless disabled in this browser. Google receives usage information and uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits. Advertising features and Google Signals are disabled. Page URL query strings and fragments are omitted, and analytics cookies have a maximum configured lifetime of 180 days without automatic renewal.

<button type="button" id="analytics-opt-out">Disable analytics in this browser</button>

Your choice is saved locally in this browser. Previous refusals are respected. Blocking browser storage also disables analytics. This does not delete information already sent to Google; existing cookies can be removed in browser settings.

Google may process data outside your country. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). GitHub Pages hosts this site; external fonts and embedded YouTube videos may independently connect to their providers.

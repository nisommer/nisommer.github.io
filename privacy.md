## Optional audience measurement

This personal website uses Google Analytics 4 only if you select **Accept** in the analytics notice. It helps Nicolas Sommer understand which pages people visit and how the website is used. If you refuse, the site remains available and this website does not load Google Analytics or send analytics events to Google.

After acceptance, Google receives information about your visit, including page addresses, device/browser information and a cookie-based identifier. Your IP address is necessarily used to make the network connection. Google Analytics processes it to derive approximate location and says it does not log or store individual IP addresses. This implementation omits query strings and fragments from the initial page address and referrer sent to Analytics. Do not put personal or sensitive information into page addresses.

Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled by this website's tag. The tag denies advertising storage and advertising-related consent. This is audience measurement, not an advertising feature.

## Your choice and cookies

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser's local storage for up to six months. After that, you will be asked again. If browser storage is unavailable, acceptance applies only to the current page and the site may ask again on another visit. No analytics is enabled by default.

If accepted, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies named `_ga` and `_ga_…` for up to 180 days; this tag does not refresh their expiry on each visit. These identifiers help distinguish visits. Browser restrictions or Google's processing may shorten their effective duration.

Use **Analytics preferences** in the footer at any time, then **Refuse**, to withdraw consent. This stops collection on the current page, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site and reloads the page without Analytics. Withdrawal does not delete data already sent to Google. Preferences are specific to the browser and site hostname you use; clearing browser data also clears your saved choice.

## Other third-party content

The site also includes external resources, such as fonts and embedded YouTube videos. They may connect to their providers independently of this analytics choice. This analytics preference controls Google Analytics only, not those resources or the website hosting service.

Google may process information on servers outside your country. Read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for further details. You may also use [Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
